Understanding Dynamic Priority

The main idea behind your project.

01

Start with a Priority

Every process enters the ready queue with an initial priority given by the user.

02

Track Waiting

The scheduler observes how long each process has been waiting for CPU time.

03

Increase Priority

After a process waits for the aging interval, its priority is increased.

04

Reduce Starvation

A process with a low initial priority can eventually become important enough to run.
